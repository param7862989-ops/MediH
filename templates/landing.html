THREE PILLARS OF CLINICAL EXCELLENCE

Bridging Advanced AI with Absolute Clinical Trust

Engineered specifically to address black-box AI rejection, hospital data silos, and late patient decompensation.

🩺

1. Multimodal Risk Assessment

Synthesizes continuous vital signs, hemodynamic formulas (Mean Arterial Pressure, Shock Index), 72-hour least-squares trajectory slopes, and laboratory biomarkers across 30 engineered features.

  • K-Means Clustering: Stratifies patients into 4 clinical risk tiers (Low, Medium, High, Critical).
  • Decision Tree Explainability: Unscaled physiological rules (e.g., Glucose ≤ 111.25 mg/dL) provide clinicians with transparent, human-in-the-loop logic.
  • KNN Precedents (K=5): Matches patient presentations against historical cohort cases with empirical therapy response insights.
  • Neural Network Inference: Dual-head deep model assisting in early sepsis detection and 30-day readmission risk estimation.

2. Sovereign Patient Medical Vault

Empowers patients with ownership of their personal health data. Records are held in a secure, patient-governed repository rather than locked inside isolated institutional EMR silos.

  • 7 Medical Categories: Allergies, Medications, Lab Reports, Surgeries, Chronic Conditions, Immunizations, and Clinical Notes.
  • Granular Record Uploads: Direct entry and file attachments with structured clinical parameters.
  • Zero Standing Access: No facility possesses ambient permanent access; every viewing requires explicit patient-granted authorization.
  • Dual-Stream Audit Trail: Immutable logging of all access attempts visible directly to the patient.
Access Patient Vault →
🔑

3. Cryptographic QR Sharing

Share authorized medical summaries in seconds without administrative delay or data leakage. Generates signed, tamper-evident QR passes with strict category whitelists.

  • RFC 7519 Signed JWTs: Signed using HMAC-SHA256 with scoped permissions, expiration epochs, and unique pass hashes.
  • Zero Data Leakage: If a patient authorizes only Allergies & Medications, sensitive records remain cryptographically sealed.
  • 1-Click Instant Revocation: Patients can revoke any active pass immediately, barring subsequent provider scans.
  • Bedside Optical Scanning: Fast in-browser decoding via OpenCV and live camera viewfinder.

ABOUT MEDIHAVEN

Ethical AI in Modern Inpatient Healthcare

Bridging high-frequency clinical telemetry with physician trust and patient data sovereignty.

🔬

Our Clinical Mission

Hospital-acquired acute deterioration and sepsis remain primary contributors to preventable ICU transfers. MediHaven provides actionable, non-black-box clinical decision support that alerts care teams days before hemodynamic collapse, enabling timely bedside intervention.

🧠

Explainable Multi-Model Consensus

Rather than opaque deep networks alone, MediHaven fuses unsupervised K-Means risk clustering, O(d) physiological Decision Trees, KNN precedent retrieval, and dual-head neural networks. Every prediction is paired with human-interpretable biomarker rules.

🛡️

Zero-Trust Patient Sovereignty

Patients retain absolute jurisdiction over their personal health files. Through cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 tokens and granular category whitelisting, patients authorize only the records needed for immediate clinical care, backed by immutable audit trails.
